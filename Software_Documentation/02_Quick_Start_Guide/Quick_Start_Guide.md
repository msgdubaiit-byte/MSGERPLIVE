## 2. Centralized Solo Hub (Fastest Way to Work)

The **Centralized Solo Hub** is your primary day-to-day command center. It unifies order intake, stock check, HSN classification, Excel deliverables, and watermarked MTC certificates into **1 Single Screen**.

![Figure 2: Centralized Solo Hub Operations Desk](file:///C:/Users/Admin/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/msg-oilfield-erp/Software_Documentation/05_Screenshots/03_Solo_Hub/Fig_3_1_Centralized_Solo_Hub.png)
*Figure 2: Centralized Solo Hub Operations Desk*

### How to Process an Order in 3 Simple Steps:
1. **Select or Upload Order**: Choose an existing Sales Order from the dropdown or upload a customer's PDF PO.
2. **Verify Tariff HSN Codes**: Review the live HSN table below the order selector. The system automatically detects Stainless Steel (`7307.2100`) and Carbon Steel (`7307.9100`).
3. **1-Click Complete Package**: Click the amber **Generate Complete Deliverables Package (ZIP)** button. The system generates Client Summary Excel, Stores Preparation Sheet, CIPL Commercial Invoice, and watermarked MTC PDFs into a single ZIP file.

## 3. Checking Real-Time Stock & Heat Lots

Click **Master Stock** in the left sidebar navigation.

![Figure 3: Master Stock Live Balance Ledger](file:///C:/Users/Admin/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/msg-oilfield-erp/Software_Documentation/05_Screenshots/05_Stock_Inventory/Fig_5_1_Master_Stock_Inventory.png)
*Figure 3: Master Stock Live Balance Ledger*

- **Physical Pieces**: Actual material in warehouse racks.
- **Reserved Pieces**: Committed to accepted customer purchase orders.
- **Available Pieces**: Free inventory ready for immediate quotation or dispatch.

$$\text{Available} = \text{Physical} - \text{Reserved}$$

## 4. Physical Dispatch & Delivery Notes

Once items are approved by the warehouse storekeeper, navigate to **Dispatch Management**.

![Figure 4: Outbound Dispatch Management & Delivery Notes](file:///C:/Users/Admin/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/msg-oilfield-erp/Software_Documentation/05_Screenshots/10_Dispatch_Logistics/Fig_10_1_Dispatch_Management_Console.png)
*Figure 4: Outbound Dispatch Management & Delivery Notes*

1. Verify Customer Name, Driver Name, and Vehicle Plate number.
2. Review line items and associated heat numbers.
3. Click **Generate Delivery Note (.pdf)** to print the signed delivery note.
4. Click **Confirm Dispatch** to commit the physical deduction to the permanent inventory ledger.
